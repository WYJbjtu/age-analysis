# Figure captions

**Figure1 — Regional age-selective domestic migration and population aging.**
All47 Japanese prefectures and51 U.S. state-level regions in each displayed year;
2022 is primary and2019 a separate companion. Horizontal axis: Member1's verified
resident share aged65+ (%). Vertical axis: age-specific net domestic migration
per1,000 same-age residents, for20–24 and65+. Each point has equal regional weight;
Pearson and Spearman coefficients use every valid region. Japan counts registered
inter-prefectural moves for all nationalities during the calendar year, divided
by October1 population. The original2019 annual population vintage differs from
the revised primary aging stock;2022 counts/stock are from their respective annual
releases. Japan's displayed denominators are rounded to1,000 persons. U.S. ACS
1-year B07001 inflows minus B07401 outflows are divided by B07001 current-resident
age totals. U.S. vertical bars show approximate90% ratio MOEs including denominator
uncertainty and omitting unavailable covariances; hollow points' intervals include
zero. No region is dropped for an uncertain direction. Labels are Tokyo/DC and
minimum/maximum young-adult rate point estimates in that country/year. Panels use
separate scales, without a pooled trend or a causal interpretation.

**Figure2 — What total net migration conceals.**
Complete native age profiles for pre-specified Tokyo prefecture(JP-13) and the
minimum/maximum2022 young-adult-rate U.S. cases, selected from all51 regions and
retained in2019: New Hampshire and Washington, D.C. Bars are net counts in
thousands, not rates; colors distinguish2019 and2022. Japanese age-unknown
residuals remain visible as their own category; published totals are displayed
separately, never added to the age bars. Native age definitions differ across
countries. U.S. bars carry approximate90% net-count MOEs, omitting unavailable
inflow/outflow covariance; ranking uncertainty is not estimated. Published total-cell
MOEs are combined separately and shown below the U.S. panels. Both 2022 total
intervals include zero: total signs describe point estimates only. Totals cover all
ages in Japan and ages1+ in ACS. Independent count scales are labeled. DC is a
federal district and New Hampshire is a state; neither is asserted to have the
same geography as Tokyo's urban core. Sources: Japan annual Internal Migration
files2019/2022 and U.S. ACS1-year detailed Summary Files B07001/B07401.

**Appendix — Full regional profiles, ages20+.**
Country-separated heatmaps contain every47/51 region in both years, with rows
ordered by2022 aging share (ties by region_id). The non-overlapping columns are
20–24,25–29,30–34,35–64,65+. They exclude younger and unknown ages; full native
totals remain available in the data. Cells are net migration per1,000 same-age
residents. Each country's two years share a symmetric zero-centered color scale
spanning all observed rates; country scales differ and no extremes are clipped.
Gray denotes missing values (none in these analytical cells). U.S. hollow circles
mark approximate90% rate intervals including zero. Japan has no ACS-style sampling
MOE, but registration and population-estimation limitations remain. Sources and
denominators are the same as Figure1.
