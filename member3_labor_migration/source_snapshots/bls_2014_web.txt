L322:     Annual revisions. Labor force and unemployment data for prior years reflect
L323:     adjustments made at the end of each year. The adjusted estimates incorporate
L324:     updated population controls from the U.S. Census Bureau, any revisions in the
L325:     other data sources, and model re-estimation. The population controls (except for
L326:     Puerto Rico) reflect extrapolation from the 2010 Census. In most years, historical
L327:     data for the most recent 5 years (both seasonally adjusted and not seasonally
L328:     adjusted) are revised near the beginning of each calendar year, prior to the
L329:     release of January estimates. Though the labor force estimates typically are
L330:     updated for 5 years, the population estimates are adjusted back to the decennial
L331:     estimates base (April 2010). With the introduction of a new generation of times-
L332:     series models in 2015, historical data were re-estimated back to January 1976 for
L333:     regions, divisions, states, the District of Columbia, the Los Angeles-Long Beach-
L334:     Glendale metropolitan division, New York City, and the balances of California and
L335:     New York states.
L336: 
L337:     Reliability of the estimates
L338: 
L339:     The estimates presented in this release are based on sample surveys, administrative
L340:     data, and modeling and, thus, are subject to sampling and other types of errors.
L341:     Sampling error is a measure of sampling variability--that is, variation that occurs
L342:     by chance because a sample rather than the entire population is surveyed. Survey
L343:     data also are subject to nonsampling errors, such as those which can be introduced
L344:     into the data collection and processing operations. Estimates not directly derived
L345:     from sample surveys are subject to additional errors resulting from the specific
L346:     estimation processes used. In table 1, level estimates for states may not sum to
L347:     level estimates for regions and divisions because of rounding. Unemployment rates
L348:     and employment-population ratios are computed from unrounded levels and, thus, may
L349:     differ slightly from rates and ratios computed using the rounded level estimates
L350:     displayed in table 1.
