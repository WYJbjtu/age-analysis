# Poster-ready Member 4 block

## Aggregation changes the interpretation

**Does the pooled relationship also hold within each country?**

In 2022, aging and manufacturing GDP share are positively correlated across all 98 regions (Pearson r=0.644). Within-country correlations are close to zero: **U.S. r=0.003 (n=51)** and **Japan r=0.029 (n=47)**. The 2019 comparison gives the same broad pattern.

**99.1% of pooled covariance lies between country means.** This accounting result does not show that aging causes industrial differences. There is no defensible direction-reversal headline; the small within-country estimates are sensitive to region omission and weighting.

Each region has equal weight. U.S. GDP is calendar-year and Japan's fiscal-year; population reference dates also differ. The countries' regional aging ranges do not overlap. Cases are selected within countries, and labor/migration evidence supplies context rather than proof of a mechanism.
